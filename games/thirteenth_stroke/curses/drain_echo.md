Whoever answers the whisper in the drain on Crooked Lane catches the Drain-Echo: from then on, everything they say ends with a wet little echo of ", down in the drain", whether they mean it to or not. It's harmless, faintly damp, and deeply embarrassing.

## How it shows
Grown-ups go pale and cross the street. Pip snorts and then looks worried. Mister Tibbersnatch finds it delightful and tries to rhyme with it. Nell Fizzlewick knows it at once and reaches for the peppermint.

## The cure
Nell Fizzlewick at the sweet shop cures it for free with a peppermint gargle, for anyone who asks.

## GM only
- It starts in Crooked Lane, if the player answers the whisper in the drain: the room's Details say `flag set drain_answered`.
- Nell cures it when asked: `flag set drain_hushed`.
