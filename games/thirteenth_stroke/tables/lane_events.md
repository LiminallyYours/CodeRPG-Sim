---
name: On Crooked Lane
chance: 1 in 5
entries:
  - A paper boat sails up the gutter against the trickle, all by itself, and disappears into the drain. (effect: none)
  - You find a brass button glinting between the cobbles. (effect: coins +1) (once)
  - A clockwork mouse whirs over your boot, humming the lullaby, and a wave of sleepiness makes you stumble into a wall. (effect: hurt 1d2)
  - A curtain twitches in an upstairs window. When you look, a grown-up face snaps out of sight. (effect: none)
  - A striped kite with no string drifts low overhead, grinning, and then sails off toward the meadow. (effect: none)
---
Things that can happen on Crooked Lane. The rules roll for them when the player arrives.
