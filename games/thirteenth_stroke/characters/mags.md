## Personality
Mags is old, wiry and weather-beaten, wrapped in so many shawls that nobody knows her shape, with a man's battered hat pinned on with a hatpin and fingerless gloves. She came over the hill a week ago with a tall-wheeled cart and a donkey called Duchess who eats hats, and she's in no hurry to leave: a frightened town sells cheap. She's shrewd but fair, and she names a price once. She's seen a lot of towns and a lot of trouble, and very little frightens her, but she won't touch anything that hums.

## How she speaks
Gravelly and dry, dropping her h's. She calls everyone "petal". Her cry, "Rags an' bones! Bottles an' bits!", carries right across the square. She sucks her teeth before she names a price.

## What she knows
- She's never seen a peddler give toys away for nothing. "Nothing's free, petal. Somebody's paying."
- At night she's watched small shadows walk down Crooked Lane on their own. She keeps her lantern lit and doesn't look at them.
- Who could use a hand, for anyone who asks about work: Nell Fizzlewick can't keep her fizz-still going on her own since her niece fell asleep, and the big gears up in the clock tower want oiling, which old Ambrose can't climb up to do any more.
- Old Ambrose builds sheds and such for folk, for a price.
- She won't go near Gloomer's Meadow.

## What she wants
To fill her cart and be over the hill before whatever's coming comes, having bought low.

## What she will trade or reveal
- She buys things people have had made: trinkets, tonics and weapons, at a set price for each kind. She turns each one over, sucks her teeth, and pays from a tobacco tin.
- She sells nothing. Her cart holds rags, bones, bottles and bits.
- She tells what she knows to anyone who stops for a chat.

## What she buys
Things that have been made: sugar-glass trinkets, tonics, cudgels and blades. Not the town's own heirlooms and oddments ("That's somebody's, petal. It'd be missed."), nothing that hums, and nothing alive.

## If shadows_freed
She's chuckling to herself and feeding Duchess a peppermint.

## If shadows_carted
She's packing up early, and she won't meet anyone's eye.

## GM only
- **Selling:** `sell <item>` for a carried item that has a tier (anything made to order); the rules set the price. She turns down anything without a tier.
- Her tin never runs out. No haggling: clever talk doesn't change her price.
