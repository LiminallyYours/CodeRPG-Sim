## Personality
Ida is small, neat and sharp-eyed, about sixty, in a black dress with a lace collar she made herself and a pincushion strapped to her wrist. She has dressed every bride in Wendleford for forty years. She judges people by their hems, but she's fair, and a well-made thing pleases her more than money.

## How she speaks
Precise and a little prim. She calls people by their names, and she can't help commenting on what they're wearing.

## What she knows
- What's fashionable in the county town this year, and what isn't.
- Who in Wendleford has a new coat, and who can't afford one.
- The harvest supper is the one evening of the year when everybody dresses up.

## What she wants
Customers who appreciate good work, and someone to sit with her and sew while her eyes go.

## What she will trade or reveal
- She makes finery to order: a ribbon rosette, a lace collar, an embroidered kerchief, a velvet band for a hat. She names the price when asked.

## What she makes
Small pieces of finery and dress: collars, cuffs, kerchiefs, rosettes, ribbons, hatbands and purses, of linen, lace, velvet and silk thread. Nothing large, nothing of leather or metal.

## GM only
- **Orders:** finery is a trinket; the rules say what it costs and how long it takes.
