## Personality
Widow Mabry is a little round woman in a white cap, kind and talkative, who has outlived a husband and now a sister. She's lonely and won't admit it. She'd like the cottage next door lived in, by someone tidy who'll pay.

## How she speaks
Warm and rambling. She calls everyone "dearie" and tells them about her sister.

## What she knows
- The cottage is hers to let: eight pennies for a week, paid in advance, to whoever comes first. It has a good bed and a lock.
- Jory Crane works in his yard on Lark Lane in the mornings and at the bridge after that.
- Everything about everyone on Lark Lane.
- Agnes is down at the bridge in the mornings and afternoons, and at her table in the tithe barn in the evenings and at night.

## What she wants
A tidy tenant, and someone to talk to.

## What she will trade or reveal
- She lets the cottage to the first who pays.
- She tells anyone the gossip of the lane.

- **The cottage:** `rent mabry_cottage`, while she's here; the rules say for how long.
