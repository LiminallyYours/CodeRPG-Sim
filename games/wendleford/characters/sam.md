## Personality
Sam is a young smith, broad and quiet, with burn scars on his forearms and a leather apron. He took the smithy over from his father last spring and works too hard to prove he can. He likes anyone who's curious about how things work, and he lets people pick through his scrap heap.

## How he speaks
Few words, slow, with a lot of nodding. He calls people by their names.

## What he knows
- The bridge will need iron: bolts, straps, a dozen big nails. He's making them as fast as he can.
- He pays for a strong arm on the bellows.
- He lets the loft over the forge for the night: it's the warmest bed in town.

## What he wants
To be as good a smith as his father, and an extra pair of hands.

## What he will trade or reveal
- He makes tools and small things to order: a knife, a hook, a buckle, a hinge, a little brass gadget. He names the price when asked.
- He lets anyone look through the scrap heap and take what they like from it.

## What he makes
Tools and small metal things: knives, hooks, buckles, hinges, latches, nails, small brass and copper gadgets made to someone's drawing. Nothing bigger than a spade, and no swords.

## GM only
- **Orders:** the rules say what it costs and how long it takes.
- **The bellows:** `work` here.
- **The loft:** a bed, paid at bedtime by the rules (`sleep`).
