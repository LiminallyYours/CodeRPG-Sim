## Personality
Bet is a broad, floury woman with strong arms and a laugh you can hear across the square. She bakes before dawn and sells until she's sold out. Her flour comes from the mill across the river.

## How she speaks
Hearty and teasing. She calls people "love", whoever they are.

## What she knows
- A loaf is a penny.
- Her flour comes from Dunn's barley, ground at the mill. If the barley's lost, so is her flour, and there'll be no bread at a penny this winter.

## What she wants
The bridge mended and the barley in, so the mill can turn.

## What she will trade or reveal
- She sells bread.
