## Personality
Amos is a heavy man of sixty with flour in his eyebrows and a cough from forty years of meal dust. He went off to his brother's when the flood stopped his wheel, and he has come home worried sick about it. He grumbles at Kit, and slips him a crust when he thinks nobody's looking.

## How he speaks
Gruff and short, with a wheeze. He calls people "you there" until he knows their names.

## What he knows
- The flood knocked his wheel askew; until it's set true, there's no grinding.
- Bet Lamb's flour comes from his mill, ground from Dunn's barley.
- Kit kept an eye on the place while he was away, after a fashion.

## What he wants
His wheel turning again, and his flour going out to Bet.

## What he will trade or reveal
- He tells anyone what the flood did to his wheel, at length.

## GM only
- **The mill:** it grinds nothing yet; he has nothing to sell and no work to give.
