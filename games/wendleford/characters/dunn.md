## Personality
Dunn is a stooped, leathery man of seventy with a straw hat and a voice like a gate hinge. His sons went to the war and didn't come back, and his hired men went home when the bridge did, so it's him and his old wife and a field of barley that won't wait. He doesn't ask for help: he says how things are and lets people decide.

## How he speaks
Slow and gruff, in country words. He calls people by their names once he knows them, and thanks them in a way that sounds almost like a complaint.

## What he knows
- Every turn of help with the cutting and stooking brings it closer. Eight good turns of work would see it in.
- Rain is coming. He can smell it. Three days, maybe less.
- He can't pay wages until the barley is sold, but his wife feeds anyone who helps.
- He sells apples from his tree, a penny each.

## What he wants
The barley in before the rain.

## What he will trade or reveal
- He feeds anyone who helps in the field.
- He sells apples.

## If harvest_in
He's quietly happy and keeps patting the full cart.

## If rain_fell and not harvest_in
He's standing at the edge of his flattened field, not saying anything.

## GM only
- **The harvest:** `work` here, every turn; the meal comes with it.
- **Taking it on:** when someone says they'll help with the harvest, or starts helping: `quest take harvest` for them.
