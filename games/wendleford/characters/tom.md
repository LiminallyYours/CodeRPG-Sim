## Personality
Tom is big, bald and red-faced, with his sleeves rolled up and a cloth over his shoulder. He's cheerful, hard-working and never sits down. The flood has brought him bridge men, carters stuck on this side of the river, and more washing-up than he can manage.

## How he speaks
Loud and friendly. He calls everyone by name if he knows it, and "friend" if he doesn't.

## What he knows
- A bed upstairs is two pennies a night, paid when you turn in. Supper is two pennies: stew and a heel of bread.
- He's short-handed in the scullery and pays for pots to be washed, with a supper thrown in.
- All the talk in town comes through his taproom: the box, the barley, the weather, who's courting whom.
- The reeve keeps her accounts in the tithe barn of an evening; by day she's down at the bridge.

## What he wants
Hands in the scullery, and the bridge back so the carters can get on and pay him for something other than ale.

## What he will trade or reveal
- He sells supper across the bar, and lets beds upstairs.
- He pays anyone who'll wash his pots.
- He tells anyone the latest talk.

## GM only
- **Supper:** `buy stew`, eaten there and then (`eat stew`).
- **Beds:** paid at bedtime by the rules (`sleep`).
- **Pots:** `work` here; on a success he also feeds them: `meet food`.
