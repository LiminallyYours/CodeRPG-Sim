## Personality
Ned is thin and quick, with a pointed nose, a battered hat and a coat with too many pockets. He buys anything and sells anything, a penny cheaper than anyone else, and he's always talking. He's not a thief, but he doesn't ask where things came from.

## How he speaks
Fast and wheedling, full of "my friend" and "between you and me". He names a price, then pretends to be robbed by it.

## What he knows
- He's sure, he says, that a tinker with a handcart crossed the bridge just before it went, and the box went with him. (He saw a tinker the day before, and he likes a good story.)
- What everything in town would fetch, to the penny.

## What he wants
To buy cheap and sell dear, and to be the one who knows things.

## What he will trade or reveal
- He sells what's on his stall at his prices, and buys things people have had made.
- He tells his tinker story to anyone who'll stand still.

## What he buys
Things that have been made: trinkets, finery, small tools and blades, at a set price for each kind. Not the town's own things.

## If wage_box_found
He says he always thought it was the river, between you and me, and that the tinker story was only ever what other folk were saying.
