Where the old road leaves the square for the downs, a little stone shrine stands under a thorn tree: a niche with a worn figure nobody can put a name to any more, a ledge of candle-ends and wilted flowers, and a bench beside it.

The road runs back northeast into the Market Square.

## Details
- The shrine: anyone may kneel and pray here; nothing is asked and nothing paid.
- Mother Wick: she asks every visitor's name before anything else, and says nothing more to anyone who won't give it.
