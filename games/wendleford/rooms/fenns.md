A narrow shop with a bow window full of bolts of cloth: plain wool and linen at the back, and at the front, where the light falls, the good stuff: a length of green velvet, ribbons in every colour, a lace collar pinned out on a card. A long counter runs down one side with a brass measure set into its edge, and behind it Ida Fenn sits sewing in the window light, a mouthful of pins.

The door to the southwest opens onto the Market Square.

## Details
- The window: the velvet and the lace are not for sale as they are; Ida makes things up to order.
- The counter: Ida makes clothes and finery to order. What it costs and how long it takes, she says when asked.
