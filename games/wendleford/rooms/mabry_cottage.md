One room downstairs and one up, small and clean and smelling of lavender and old soot. There's a hearth with a kettle-hook, a scrubbed table, two chairs, and a dresser with a few plates. Up the steep stair is a bed with a patchwork quilt, under a window that looks over the gardens. The door has a good iron lock.

The green gate to the south lets out onto Lark Lane.

## Details
- The bed: whoever has the cottage sleeps here (`sleep`, from evening).
- The lock: whoever has the cottage has the key, and what they leave here stays theirs.
