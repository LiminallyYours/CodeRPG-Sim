A dark, hot shed open on the square side, with the forge glowing red at the back and a great leather bellows beside it. The anvil stands in the middle on its block, and the walls are hung with tongs, hammers, horseshoes and half-made hinges. In one corner is the scrap heap: bent iron, broken tools, old wheel-rims and odds and ends of brass and copper. A ladder goes up to a loft over the forge, where it's always warm.

The open front lets out east onto the Market Square.

## Details
- The bellows: Sam Tolley pays for someone strong to work the bellows while he's at the anvil (`work`, a Strength check).
- The loft: a cot over the forge, warm all night. Sam lets it for the night; the price is paid when someone turns in (`sleep`, from evening).
- The scrap heap: Sam doesn't mind anyone picking through it. Most of it is rust. Among it, for anyone who looks, a coil of copper wire and a cracked brass cogwheel.
- Sam makes tools and small things to order. What it costs and how long it takes, he says when asked.
- The tools on the walls: Sam's own working tools, not for sale; he makes things to order.
