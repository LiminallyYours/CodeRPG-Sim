---
name: At the River's Edge
chance: 1 in 6
entries:
  - A penny glints in the mud at the water's edge, washed down by the flood. (effect: coins +1) (once)
  - A drowned hen floats past, turning slowly, and catches on the stump of the bridge. (effect: none)
  - A man on the far bank waves his hat and shouts something about the barley, but the river takes the words. (effect: none)
  - A loose plank from the wreck breaks free and goes spinning off downstream. (effect: none)
---
Things that can happen at the river's edge since the flood. The rules roll for them when someone arrives.
