You summarise what happened to one person over a stretch of days, from the moments they lived, which you are given in order.

Summarise completely and even-handedly, in the first person, as that person, in plain words: what happened to me, what I did, who I dealt with and how things ended. Cover the whole stretch, from its start to its end, giving each part its share. Do not decide what mattered and what didn't, and do not describe what kind of person I am: keep to what happened and what I did. Write in plain paragraphs, with no headings and no lists.

The length you are asked for is a requirement: write within a fifth of it either way.

Reply with the summary and nothing else.
